Your first adventure

New to Oregon City?

Play your own character (PC), or join the crew bringing the world to life as non-player characters (NPCs). Ask the chapter which option fits your first visit.

  1. Meet the community.Join Discord, introduce yourself in new-players, and ask about your first event.
  2. Read the event instructions.Check current dates, registration, rules and character paperwork with Logistics. Do not assume older event arrangements still apply.
  3. Prepare your character and gear.Explore the culture packets, ask about possible loan equipment, and send backstory questions to Plot.

Play across the Pacific Northwest

Seattle and Oregon City offer a cross-chapter participation incentive, confirmed by the Technology Committee Chairperson.

  1. Commit to playing a PC in one chapter and NPCing the other. Notify both Logistics teams.
  2. Attend at least 50% of each chapter’s events in that capacity during your 12-month period.
  3. At the end, send the appropriate Logistics team attendance evidence from both chapters and your requested Universal Magic Item, worth up to 2,500 Treasure Points.

NPC Guilding counts; Plot participation does not. Regular event treasure is unaffected. The player tracks participation. Only events moving forward from the October 25, 2026 announcement count. Coordinate your eligibility and item request with both Logistics teams.
